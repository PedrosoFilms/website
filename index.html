04Process

From briefing to final cut.

Post-production is our specialty, and many projects start right there, with the raw footage. When a project needs more, we cover it from the first conversation. The creative direction is approved before the edit, so the film is born approved and revisions stay small.

01 / 04

Briefing

Who the client is, and who the client's client is.

  • Market position, product and who they sell to
  • For creators: who the audience is, how it behaves, its age
  • What they already make, what they want to make, what the audience expects
Diagnosis and creative direction
02 / 04

Pre-production

The whole creative universe, approved before the edit.

  • Style frames and storyboard
  • When needed, a small brand guide: colours and type
  • Everything follows the client's brand
Approved storyboard
03 / 04

Production

We are remote and do not shoot. We build whole worlds from very little.

  • From a script, an audio track or a talking head
  • Generated images and video, VFX
  • B-roll search and selection
Material ready to cut
04 / 04

Post-production

Our specialty. Hands on: the plan becomes the film.

  • Video edit, narrative edit, audio edit
  • Motion, animation and VFX
  • Colour and finishing
Final video · 24h short · 72h long
